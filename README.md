## I learnt:
- That you can route sections of a pcb the bring them together to finish
- How to do ground fill in KiCAD
- That texas instruments is a good site to look on

## Features
- Barometer (For relative height measuring)
- IMU (tracks movement and stuff)
- Battery Charger (Powered on the go)
- High speed clock and low speed clock
- Battery Screw Terminal
- Two servo pin headers
